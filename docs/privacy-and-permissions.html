Privacy and permissions

Video Controls Plus keeps your settings in your browser. While the Usage analytics switch is on, and it is on by default, usage events are sent to the team's backend with or without an account, and your IP address goes to ipapi.co for a location lookup at most once a day. A connected account also stores your account data and, with sync on, your settings.

Turn the switch off and none of the usage recording happens.

It does not record the videos you watch, their titles or their addresses, unless you turn on Watch History, which is off by default. Saved items are the other exception. A connected account keeps a video's title and address only with something you save to it or start from it: a note, a bookmark or diary entry, a watch-later item, an uploaded screenshot, extracted video details, a transcript, a flashcard saved from a video, a learning-path video, a collection item or a watch party.

This page is a plain reading of the privacy policy. The policy is the legal text. Where the two differ, the policy is the one that counts.

What stays in your browser​

Your settings, your keyboard shortcuts, the speed remembered for each video, your playback position, your filter presets and your audio settings are stored in your browser. They go to your account only when you connect the extension and turn sync on.

Audio download is processed on your device. A screenshot stays on your device unless you choose a cloud action for it, which uploads the image.

Watch limits count minutes on this device only, and their PIN is hashed and never synced. The Study timer's counts and the chapters made from a video's captions stay in this browser. GIFs, clips, time-lapses and contact sheets are made on your device and saved as downloads.

The Usage analytics switch​

Open Settings, go to the Privacy & Data tab, and find Usage analytics. It works with and without an account.

Off, it stops three things:

  1. Usage events are not sent to the team's backend.
  2. A connected account does not record which sites, features and speeds you used.
  3. Your IP address does not go to ipapi.co.

If the switch is off in this browser or in your account at the moment you connect, it ends up off in both.

Your other switches​

They are on the same tab.

SwitchDefaultWhat it controls
Third-Party Error TrackingOnCrash reports to Sentry

Third-Party Error Tracking is separate from Usage analytics, so turning one off does not turn off the other. Under Your Data on the same tab you can export or delete your data, and Account and sync has the steps.

Where your data lives​

ServiceWhat it holds

Five features contact a service of their own, each only while you use the feature: SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app), Netflix subtitle translation or dual subtitles (api.mymemory.translated.net), Netflix ratings with your own key (www.omdbapi.com), an AI summary that has to fetch a video's captions (video.google.com/timedtext), and Look up in the widget's Save a word (en.wiktionary.org), which receives only the one word you typed. The policy's section 5.9 lists one more service, for a feature these docs do not cover yet.

What is not collected​

The policy lists these as not collected: the videos you watch (unless you turn on Watch History), video content or media files apart from a screenshot you choose to upload, your browsing on pages where the extension does nothing, passwords, payment information, and the content of pages you visit apart from text you send to an AI feature yourself and what the five features above send to their own services.

Your data is not sold.

The microphone​

Speak is the only feature that uploads your voice, and the microphone is asked for the first time you use it.

WhereWhat you see
The extensionSpeak a note shows "Allow the microphone once". Press Open the microphone page, and a tab called "Allow the microphone for Speak" opens. Press Allow microphone, then choose Allow in your browser's own box. You do this once.
The Android appThe app asks for the microphone permission when you first press record in Speak.
The Firefox add-onFirefox also asks for its optional "Personal communications" data permission before anything is recorded or sent.

The microphone is on only while you record. If you block it, the page tells you where to switch it back on. Turn Speak off in Settings, on the AI Features tab, and the extension stops offering it.

What each permission is for​

The extension asks for seven permissions and for access to sites, and on Chrome and Edge for an eighth, offscreen. It does not ask for the identity permission, because there is no sign-in inside the extension. The microphone is not a permission it asks for at install.

PermissionWhy the extension asks for it
storageSaves your settings, shortcuts, speed memory, filter presets and theme in the browser
activeTabLets the popup read and change the video in the tab you are on when you open it
contextMenusAdds the right-click menu items
alarmsTimers for the sleep timer, sending queued sync and usage batches, checking a pending account connection, refreshing remote switches, lifting a per-site pause when it ends, and one daily check for an announcement notification.
notificationsShows speed changes, loop status and sleep timer alerts when the widget is hidden or the display mode is Notification Only
clipboardWriteCopies a link to your clipboard from the right-click items "Copy Page URL" and "Copy URL with Timestamp"; the clipboard is never read
scriptingBrings the controls to a video embedded inside another page, and only into a frame that contains a video
offscreen (Chrome and Edge)Lets Speak record in a hidden page of the extension and send the recording from there, so the recording keeps going while the upload runs. Firefox records in its background page and does not need it.
Access to all sitesFinds and controls the video on any site you open

Access to all sites is the broad one. The extension needs it because a video can be on any site. While a site is paused, the extension does nothing there.

On Firefox​

Firefox asks for three optional data permissions, and each one gates what is sent.

Firefox permissionWhat it gates
Technical and interaction dataUsage events are sent only if it is allowed. The Usage analytics switch asks for it.
Website contentPage content (a screenshot you upload, a transcript you save to the account, captions for an AI run, a word you save with its caption line) is sent only after you allow it. The add-on asks the first time you use one of those.
Personal communicationsA Speak recording and its transcripts are sent only after you allow it. The add-on asks the first time you use Speak.

Other apps from the same team​

Settings has a tab that lists other apps from the same team. The list comes with the extension, and nothing is fetched to show it. Announcements shown beside it are read from the team's backend. No ad network and no ad SDK are in the extension.
