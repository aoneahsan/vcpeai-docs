Your browser updates Video Controls Plus on its own, and the website does the same. You only see a message when your copy is older than a version we marked as required.

## How the extension updates

The store your browser installed it from sends new versions, and the browser installs them in the background. Video Controls Plus also asks the browser for an update when it starts, when you open the popup or Settings, and every 6 hours. When a new version has arrived, the extension reloads once the popup and Settings are closed.

## An update is ready

When we release a version you need, the popup and Settings show **An update is ready.** with the reason for that release.

1. Press **Open extension store** to update now.
2. Or press **Keep working** and carry on. The message stays until you close the popup or Settings.

The next time you open the popup or Settings, the update screen takes their place until you update.

## The update screen

**Update Video Controls Plus.** names the version you need and the reason. Press **Open extension store**, update the extension in your browser, then reopen the popup. Nothing else in the popup or Settings works until then.

An urgent release shows this screen at once, without the notice first.

## On the website

The website switches to a new build on its own, the next time you change page or come back to its tab. A required release shows the same notice there, with **Update now** and **Keep working**, and from your next visit the update screen with **Update**. Pressing **Update** loads the new version.

## Offline

Offline, the extension and the website open as usual. If you had already seen the notice, the update screen still appears, because the version you need is known.
