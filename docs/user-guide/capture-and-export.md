Capture & export makes a GIF, a clip, a time-lapse, a burst of screenshots or a contact sheet from the video, and it lets you draw on a screenshot. Open the widget's Tools tab and find **Capture & export**. It needs [a connected account](/docs/account-and-sync), like the [screenshot](/docs/user-guide/screenshots).

Everything is made on your device and saved as a download. Nothing is uploaded.

## What you can make

| Button | What you get |
|---|---|
| **GIF · next 5 s** | A looping GIF of the next 3, 5, 10 or 15 seconds, 320, 480 or 640 px wide |
| **● Record clip** | A clip with picture and sound, as WebM. Press **■ Stop clip** to end it |
| **Time-lapse 4x** | The video at 2x, 4x, 8x or 16x, picture only, as WebM |
| **Burst every 5 s** | A screenshot every 2, 5, 10 or 30 seconds while the video plays, up to 100 |
| **Contact sheet 3×3** | 3 × 3 or 4 × 4 frames from across the whole video, each with its time, as one PNG |
| **Annotate** | A screenshot you draw on |

The numbers on the buttons follow your choices in Settings. A clip and a time-lapse stop by themselves after 10 minutes, or when the video ends.

When a burst is on, the button shows how many shots it has. Save them with **Save … as ZIP** (the button shows the count), which gives a ZIP of PNGs, or **Save as sheet**, which puts them on one image.

## Annotate a screenshot

1. Press **Annotate**. The **Annotate screenshot** panel opens on the current frame.
2. Pick a tool: Arrow, Box, Highlight, Text or Pen.
3. Pick a color: Red, Yellow, Green, Blue or White.
4. Drag on the picture to draw. With Text, click, type, then press Enter.
5. Press **Save PNG** to download it, or **Copy** to put it on the clipboard.

`Ctrl + Z` undoes the last mark. Escape closes the panel.

## Capture settings

Settings, Video Features tab, **Capture & export** sets the **GIF length**, the **GIF width**, the **Time-lapse speed**, how often a burst fires (**Burst every**) and the **Contact sheet** size. **Reset capture settings** puts the defaults back.

## When it does not work

Protected video, such as Netflix and Disney+, cannot be captured, and some other sites do not allow their frames to be saved. The widget tells you which it is. A contact sheet needs a video with a known length, so it does not work on a live stream. A paused video cannot be captured, so press play first.

## Shortcuts

Three optional shortcuts start with no key: "Record or stop a clip", "Make a GIF" and "Annotate a screenshot". Each needs a connected account. Set them up in [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).

To share a clip shaped for social posts, see [Share and watch together](/docs/user-guide/share-and-watch-together).
