The filters are brightness, contrast and saturation from 0% to 200%, plus grayscale, invert and night mode. With a connected account ([what connecting adds](/docs/account-and-sync)), press `Ctrl + Alt + F` to open the Filters tab in the widget.

## The controls

| Control | Range or effect | Key |
|---|---|---|
| Filters tab | Opens and closes the Filters tab | `Ctrl + Alt + F` |
| Brightness | 0% to 200% | Slider |
| Contrast | 0% to 200% | Slider |
| Saturation | 0% to 200% | Slider |
| Grayscale | On or off | `Ctrl + Alt + G` |
| Invert | On or off | `Ctrl + Alt + I` |
| Night mode | On or off | `Ctrl + Alt + T` |

100% is the picture as the site sends it. Below 100% is less, above is more, and each slider stops at 200%. **Reset All Filters** on the same tab puts everything back.

## When each one helps

A screen recording with pale gray text on white is hard to read. Raise the contrast.

On a bright afternoon a dark video is hard to see. Raise the brightness.

A whiteboard lecture late at night is a white rectangle in a dark room, so press `Ctrl + Alt + I` to invert it, or `Ctrl + Alt + T` for night mode.

## Presets

Set the three sliders the way you want them and save the result as a preset on the Filters tab. You can save up to 20 presets, and since presets are part of your settings, they can be included when you [export your settings](/docs/account-and-sync).

## More picture controls

Two other widget tabs change the picture, and neither one is the Filters tab.

| Where | Control | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| View tab, **Transform** | Rotate, mirror, flip | Rotates the picture 90 degrees, mirrors it horizontally or flips it vertically |
| Tools tab, **Visual Modes** | Ambient | Ambient light around the player |
| Tools tab, **Visual Modes** | Cinema | A cinema color preset |
| Tools tab, **Visual Modes** | Letterbox | Cinema bars above and below the picture |

Filters change how the video is drawn in your browser. A screenshot or a download is a separate control.
