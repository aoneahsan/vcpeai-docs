Audio boost raises the volume beyond 100%, up to 400%, after you [sign in](/docs/account-and-sync). Press `Ctrl + Alt + U` to raise the boost and `Ctrl + Alt + Y` to lower it.

## The audio keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl + Alt + U` | Boost up by 20% |
| `Ctrl + Alt + Y` | Boost down by 20% |
| `Ctrl + Alt + N` | Normalize on or off |

The same controls are on the widget's Audio tab.

## Boost

A quiet recording, such as a lecture recorded from the back of a room, stays quiet at the player's 100%, and boost takes the volume past that limit, between 100% and 400%.

Each press moves it by 20%. At 400% a loud video is very loud, so raise it a little at a time.

## Normalize

Normalize reduces the dynamic range: the gap between the loudest and the quietest parts gets smaller. Turn it on with `Ctrl + Alt + N` for a video where the speaker whispers and the intro music does not.

## Volume memory

The extension remembers the volume and the mute state per site. Set YouTube quiet and a course site loud, and each one comes back the way you left it. The **Remember Volume** card is in Settings, on the Video Features tab.

## More audio controls

It does a lot more with the sound than make it louder: bass boost, noise reduction, vocal isolation and five more are each one button under **Advanced Audio** on the widget's Audio tab. The tab asks for a sign-in, so you reach them after signing in.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Bass | Bass boost |
| Denoise | Noise reduction, which filters background noise |
| Spatial | Spatial audio |
| Vocals | Vocal isolation |
| Compress | The audio compressor |
| Karaoke | Karaoke mode |
| Pitch | The pitch preset |
| EQ | Equalizer presets |

## Audio download

Audio download saves the audio of a video as WebM or WAV. It does not save MP3. Its switch is **Audio Download**, in Settings on the Download & Capture tab, and the audio is processed on your device.
