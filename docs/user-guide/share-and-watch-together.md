**Share & watch together** on the widget's Tools tab shares a link at the current moment and records a clip shaped for social posts. It also starts a watch party. Sharing a moment needs no account, while a social clip and a watch party need [a connected account](/docs/account-and-sync), and in a party every person who joins needs one.

## Share this moment

Press **Share this moment**. The panel shows the link at the current time, the same link `Ctrl + Alt + X` copies.

- **Copy link** puts it on your clipboard.
- The **QR code** is made on your device. **Save QR code** saves it as a PNG.
- The buttons below send it to X, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit or Email.

## Clip for social

Pick a **Clip shape**: Original, Square 1:1 or Vertical 9:16. Then press **Clip for social**. It records with the [clip recorder](/docs/user-guide/capture-and-export), cropped to the center of the picture, and **■ Stop clip** ends it. It does not work on protected video.

## Start a watch party

1. Open the video you want to watch together.
2. Press **Start a watch party**.
3. Press **Copy invite link**, or share the 10-character party code.
4. A friend who gets the invite link sees a website page called "You are invited to watch together" and presses **Open the video and join**. A friend with the code opens the same video, types it under **Party code** and presses **Join**.

While the party runs, the widget shows who is there and the code. Play, pause, a jump to a new time and the speed stay in step for everyone, up to 20 people.

**Leave** takes you out. **End the party** is for the host, and a party also ends by itself after 6 hours. One person can have up to 3 parties running at a time.

There is no chat. Use whatever you already talk in.

## What a party sends and keeps

The live sync runs through Supabase Realtime and only the party's members receive it. The play, pause, jump and speed messages are not stored.

The party itself keeps the video's address and title and the party code. It also keeps who joined and when. The [privacy page](/docs/privacy-and-permissions) has the details.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "This watch party has ended or the code is not right." | Check the code, or ask the host for a new one |
| "This watch party is full (20 people)." | The party has reached 20 people |
| "You already have 3 watch parties running. End one first." | End a party before you start another |
| "Connect your account to use watch parties." | Connect an account, then try again |
| "A party code is 10 letters and numbers." | The code you typed is the wrong length or has other characters |
