You choose how the controls appear on the page in Settings, on the General tab under **Display Mode**. It works signed out.

## Display modes

| Mode | What you see on the page |
|---|---|
| Floating Widget | The widget, a control panel you can drag anywhere on the page. This is the default. |
| Notification Only | A small speed indicator when the speed changes, and no panel |

The widget's Tools tab has the same choice under **Display & Theme**, where the two modes are labeled **Floating** and **Notify**.

Notification Only suits you when you drive everything from the keyboard, you already know your keys, and you want nothing sitting on top of the page while the video plays.

## Widget settings

These are on the General tab, under the display mode.

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Widget Position | Top Left, Top Right, Bottom Left, Bottom Right |
| Widget Size | Normal, Compact, Minimal |
| Open Widget On | Click, Hover |

Out of the box the widget is Normal size and opens on a click.

## Hide the widget

Press `Esc` or `Ctrl + Alt + H` to hide the widget, and press again to show it.

To switch the whole extension off on one site, use [Pause per site](/docs/getting-started/pause-per-site).

## Change the theme

Open the Appearance tab in Settings.

| Option | How many |
|---|---|
| Color schemes | 20 |
| Font families | 11 |
| Border radius options | 6 |
| Font size scales | 5 |

There is also a compact mode and a reduced motion switch. The 11 fonts come with the extension, and none of them is loaded from a remote font service.

## Change the language

Settings and the popup are available in English, Spanish, French and German: **Auto-detect Language** follows your browser's language, and **Language** lets you pick one yourself.

The widget is in English today. It stays in English when Settings is in Spanish, French or German.

## Related keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Esc` | Hide or show the widget |
| `Ctrl + Alt + H` | Hide or show the widget |
| `\` | Show the current speed |

The starting keys are listed in [Default shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/defaults), and [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding) shows how to move them.
