Automation blocks autoplay, keeps a speed for each site, ramps the speed up slowly and reminds you to watch a video later. Open the widget's Playback tab and find **Automation**. None of it needs an account.

Settings, Video Features tab, **Playback automation** lists your blocked and allowed sites, your site speeds and your reminders, with **Remove** beside each one.

## Block autoplay

Press **Block autoplay here** and videos on this site wait until you press play. A held video shows "Autoplay blocked — press play to watch".

To block it on every site, turn on **Block autoplay everywhere** in Settings and add the sites you want to keep under **Sites where autoplay is allowed**. Tick **Tell me when a blocked video is ready** for a notice when a held video in a hidden tab is ready to play.

## Keep a speed for a site

Set the speed you like, then press **Keep 1.5x on this site** (the button shows your current speed). Videos on that site now start at it. A speed the extension remembers for one video or one playlist still wins.

## Speed ramp

A speed ramp raises the speed bit by bit while the video plays. Pick where it ends, from 1.25x to 2.5x, and how long it takes, from 5 to 30 minutes, then press **Start**. It moves in 0.1x steps. Press **Stop** to end it.

## Remind me to watch this

Press **Remind me to watch this** and pick **In 1 hour**, **Tonight 20:00**, **Tomorrow 08:00** or **Pick a date and time…**, then **Repeat** it **Once**, **Daily** or **Weekly**, and press **Set**. A browser notification arrives at that time. Click it to open the video. On YouTube it opens at the moment you saved. You can have up to 50 reminders at a time.

## One video at a time

In Settings, Video Features tab, turn on **Pause videos in other tabs when one starts**. When a video starts, videos playing in your other tabs of this browser pause. It is off until you turn it on, and it needs no account.

## Up next from Watch Later

This one needs [a connected account](/docs/account-and-sync). It starts off. In Settings, Video Features tab, turn on **Play my Watch Later in order** and pick a **Countdown** of 5, 10 or 15 seconds. When a video ends, a card over it counts down to the next video in your Watch Later list, or to the first one when the video you finished is not in the list, with **Play now** and **Cancel**. After the last video, nothing plays. Replaying or seeking back cancels it, and a looping video never ends, so it never moves on.

## Resume from another device

With **Remember Playback Position** on, a connected account and Watch History turned on, the extension can pick up where another device stopped. It asks: "You got to 12:40 on another device. Resume?" It does this only when this browser has no saved position of its own, and not near the very end of the video.

## Shortcuts

"Block autoplay on this site" and "Start or stop the speed ramp" start with no key. Give them one in [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).
