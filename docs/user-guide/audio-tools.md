Audio tools change the balance, the voice, the room and the loudness of the video you are watching, and they sit on the widget's Audio tab, which needs [a connected account](/docs/account-and-sync) like the rest of that tab. They change only what you hear on the page.

They do not work on protected video, such as Netflix and Disney+. The widget says so.

## Balance

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left / right** | Moves the sound toward the left or the right speaker; **Centre** is the middle |
| **Mono** | Plays both channels as one |
| **Swap L/R** | Swaps the left and right channels |

## Voice & room

Pick a **Voice**: Chipmunk, Deep, Robot, Echo or Telephone. Each one is a real pitch shift, and the video speed never changes. The Pitch button under Advanced Audio is the Deep voice.

Pick a **Room**: Small room, Hall, Cathedral or Cave. **Room mix** sets how much of the room you hear.

**Off** under either one puts it back.

## Loudness

**Even loudness** brings quiet parts up and loud parts down, by at most 12 dB. Turn it on for a video where the speaker is soft and the music is not.

**Limiter** stops the sound from going past a **Ceiling** you set, from -1 dB to -12 dB. It keeps a sudden loud moment in check.

**Turn audio tools off** switches all of them off at once.

## Audio tools settings

Settings, Video Features tab, **Audio tools** holds the same controls: **Balance (left / right)**, **Mono**, **Swap left and right**, **Voice**, **Room**, **Room mix**, **Even loudness**, **Limiter** and **Limiter ceiling**. **Reset audio tools** puts them back.

## When it does not work

The widget shows one of three messages:

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Not available on protected video (Netflix, Disney+ and similar): its audio cannot be processed." | The site protects the video, and the audio cannot be changed |
| "Not available on this video: the site does not let its audio be processed." | The video comes from another site that does not allow it |
| "Starts when the video plays." | Press play and the tools start |

[Audio boost](/docs/user-guide/audio-boost) and normalize are separate controls on the same tab, with their own keys and their own page, which also covers the eight Advanced Audio buttons.

## Shortcuts

Two optional shortcuts start with no key: "Turn audio tools on or off" and "Swap left and right". Each needs a connected account. Give one a key in [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).
