Press `Ctrl + Alt + C` to save the current frame as PNG or JPEG; it needs a [sign-in](/docs/account-and-sync).

## Take a screenshot

1. Pause on the frame you want, or leave the video playing.
2. Press `Ctrl + Alt + C`, or click **Capture** on the widget's Tools tab.
3. The frame is downloaded, copied or uploaded, depending on the action you chose in Settings.

Pause, press `,` or `.` to step one frame at a time, and you capture the exact frame where the slide is complete.

## Choose the format and where the image goes

Open Settings, go to the Video Features tab and find the **Screenshot** card.

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| Format | PNG (higher quality) or JPEG (smaller file) |
| Action | Download, Copy to Clipboard, Download + Clipboard, Upload to Cloud, or Cloud + Clipboard |
| Cloud Visibility | Private or Public, for the cloud actions |
| Sync to cloud library | On or off |
| Include Timestamp | On or off |
| Timestamp Position | Top Left, Top Right, Bottom Left, Bottom Right |

The timestamp is the video time of the frame, written on the image. Turn it on for lecture slides and you know where each one came from.

## Where the image goes

Choosing a cloud action uploads the image. Otherwise the capture stays on your device.

## One frame or a moving part

A screenshot is one frame. For a moving section, the clip recorder records a part of the video and downloads it, and its switch is **Clip Recording**, in Settings on the Download & Capture tab.

## Related keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl + Alt + C` | Capture the frame |
| `,` | Step back one frame |
| `.` | Step forward one frame |

All three can be moved: see [Default shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/defaults) for the starting keys and [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding) for the steps.
