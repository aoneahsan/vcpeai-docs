Looks & overlays adds color grades, art looks, sharpening, a vignette, film grain, a text overlay and blurred regions to the video. It sits on the widget's Filters tab, which needs [a connected account](/docs/account-and-sync), like the rest of that tab. **Zoom & fit** is on the View tab and needs no account.

Every effect changes only the picture on the page, never the video file. Some streaming sites block effects on protected video, and the extension does not claim they work there.

## Looks & overlays

Open the widget's Filters tab and find **Looks & overlays**.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Colour grade** | One of 20 grades, such as Teal & orange, Film noir, Vintage 8mm, Golden hour and Moonlight, with a **Strength** slider |
| **Art look** | Oil painting, Pencil sketch, Engraving or Coloured emboss |
| **Sharpen** | 0% to 100%, in steps of 10, applied before the color tools |
| **Vignette** | Darkens the corners of the picture |
| **Film grain** | Adds grain, still or moving |
| **Text** | Puts your **Overlay text** in a corner of the video |
| **Blur a region** | Drag a box on the video to blur that part |
| **Turn all effects off** | Puts the picture back |

A blurred region stays until you remove it with **Remove blurred regions** or the video changes. No video pixels are read. The blur happens on the page.

## Effects settings

Settings, Video Features tab, **Effects** holds the finer options: **Vignette strength**, **Grain strength**, **Moving grain**, **Overlay text** and **Text corner** (Top left, Top right, Bottom left or Bottom right). **Reset effects** returns them to their defaults.

Effects work with [Video filters](/docs/user-guide/video-filters) and the [Comfort tools](/docs/user-guide/comfort-and-accessibility) at the same time, and they follow the video into fullscreen.

## Zoom & fit

Open the widget's View tab and find **Zoom & fit**. No account needed.

1. Press **Zoom in** or **Zoom out**. The zoom goes from 1x to 4x in quarter steps, and pinching on a trackpad works too.
2. Use the four arrows to move the view left, up, down or right.
3. Turn on **Magnifier: follow the pointer** and the zoomed part follows your mouse.
4. Pick a **Fit**: Original, Fill (crop the bars), Stretch, 4:3, 16:9 or 21:9.

**Back to the original picture** resets it. Zoom works with rotate, mirror and flip, and **Reset Transform** leaves the zoom where it is. A new video starts at Original, and nothing here is stored.

## Shortcuts

Two optional shortcuts start with no key: "Turn effects on or off" and "Blur a region of the video". Both need a connected account when pressed. Set them in [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).
