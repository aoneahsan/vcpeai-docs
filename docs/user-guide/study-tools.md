The Study timer runs focus rounds and breaks, the transcript panel shows the captions a video already has beside it, and **Make chapters with AI** turns those captions into chapters. The timer and the transcript need no account.

## Study timer

Open the widget's Smart tab and find **Study timer**.

1. Pick a **Preset**: Classic 25/5, Short 15/3, Long focus 50/10, Study 45/10, or **Your own lengths**.
2. Press **Start**. The timer shows Focus, Break or Long break, and which round you are on.
3. Press **Pause** and **Resume** as you need, or **Skip to break**.
4. **Reset the timer** starts over.

It counts today's focus minutes against your **Daily focus goal** and shows a day streak. One timer runs for the whole browser. Its counts stay in this browser.

Settings, Video Features tab, **Study timer** sets the lengths, **Long break after (rounds)** and the goal. It can also **Pause the video during breaks** and **Play it again when the break ends**, with a **Sound at the end of a round** or a **Browser notification at the end of a round**.

## Transcript

Press **Show transcript** on the widget's Tools tab. The panel opens beside the widget with the captions the video already has, and the line playing now is highlighted.

- Click a line to jump there.
- Type in **Search the transcript** to find a line.
- **Follow the video** keeps the playing line in view.
- **Copy all** copies the whole transcript.

It works on videos that carry captions. A video with none shows "This video has no captions, so there is no transcript." The extension does not make captions from the audio.

## Chapters from the captions

In the transcript panel, press **Make chapters with AI**. One AI run turns the captions into 3 to 15 chapters. It uses the same route as the AI summary: your Shared allowance with a connected account, your own key, or a local model ([AI features](/docs/ai-features)).

Click a chapter to jump to it. The playing chapter is marked. **Copy chapters** gives "0:00 Title" lines, and **Make again** spends a new run. Chapters are kept for each video in this browser, for up to 50 videos.

## Practice a part

On the widget's Loop tab, **Practice** helps you learn a hard part of an [A-B loop](/docs/user-guide/ab-loop), which needs a connected account. **Pause before each pass** waits 1 to 5 seconds at point A. **Speed up each pass** adds 5% or 10% each time, up to normal speed. Set a slower speed first.

## Shortcuts

"Start or pause the study timer" and "Show or hide the transcript" start with no key. Set them in [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).
