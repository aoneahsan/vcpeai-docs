Press `Ctrl + Alt + P` to put the video in a Picture-in-Picture window, and press it again to bring it back. It works without an account.

## Use it

1. Start the video.
2. Press `Ctrl + Alt + P`.
3. Switch to another tab. The video keeps playing in its own small window.
4. Press `Ctrl + Alt + P` again to leave Picture-in-Picture.

The keys stay with it: `]` and `[` change the speed of the small window too, and the rest of the speed controls keep working while it is in Picture-in-Picture.

## Enter on its own when you scroll

On a long page the video scrolls out of view when you read the text under it. The extension can move it into Picture-in-Picture on its own when that happens.

1. Open Settings and go to the Advanced tab.
2. Find **Playback Features**.
3. Make sure **Picture-in-Picture** is on.
4. Turn on **Auto-enter PiP on Scroll**.

## Related keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl + Alt + P` | Picture-in-Picture on or off |
| `Ctrl + Alt + Z` | Theater mode on or off |
| `]` / `[` | 0.1x faster or slower, in the small window too |

To move any of them, start from [Default shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/defaults) and follow [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).
