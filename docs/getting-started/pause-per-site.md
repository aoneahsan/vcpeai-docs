title: Pause the extension on one site
sidebar_label: Pause per site
description: Pause Video Controls Plus on a single site from the popup, for 1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day, 1 week, or until you turn it back on.
---

Open the popup on the site and click **Pause on this site**. That switches the whole extension off on that one site, with no widget and no shortcuts, and nothing changes on your other sites.

## Pause a site

1. Open the site you want to pause.
2. Click the Video Controls Plus icon in the toolbar.
3. Click **Pause on this site**.
4. Pick how long.

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| Until I turn it back on | The site stays paused until you resume it |
| For 1 hour | The pause ends on its own after 1 hour |
| For 4 hours | The pause ends on its own after 4 hours |
| For 1 day | The pause ends on its own after 1 day |
| For 1 week | The pause ends on its own after 1 week |

The page reloads. While the site is paused, there is no widget on it and no shortcut runs.

## Resume a site

1. Open the paused site.
2. Open the popup. It shows **Paused** and how long is left.
3. Click **Resume**.

## Limits

The list of paused sites stays on this device and does not sync to your account, so a site you pause on your laptop is not paused on another computer.

The pause button appears only on a site where the extension is running. If the popup says **Not enabled**, there is nothing to pause.

## Related keys

Pausing has no shortcut. To hide only the widget, press `Esc` or `Ctrl + Alt + H`. Both are in [Default shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/defaults), and [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding) moves them.
