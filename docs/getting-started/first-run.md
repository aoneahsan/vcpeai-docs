Open any page with a video and press `]`. The video goes 0.1x faster, and you've used the extension.

## Try the first controls

1. Open a video on YouTube, Udemy, Vimeo or any other site with a standard video.
2. Press `]` to go 0.1x faster, and keep pressing until you reach 4x.
3. Press `[` to go 0.1x slower.
4. Press `=` to go back to your default speed.
5. Press `Ctrl + Alt + 4` to jump straight to 2x.

That's speed control, and it needs no account. The same keyboard does a lot more than speed: `,` and `.` step the video one frame at a time, and `Ctrl + Alt + P` puts it in a Picture-in-Picture window, before you connect an account.

## Find the widget

The widget is the floating control panel on the page. Click it to open it, and drag it where you want it.

Press `Esc` or `Ctrl + Alt + H` to hide the widget, and press again to bring it back.

## Find the popup and Settings

Click the Video Controls Plus icon in the toolbar to open the popup. It shows the site you're on and whether the extension is running there, and it's where you [pause the extension on one site](/docs/getting-started/pause-per-site).

Settings opens in its own tab.

## What needs a connected account

Speed, presets, frame step, Picture-in-Picture, share timestamp, theater mode, browser-local bookmarks and the widget work without an account. These controls open after you connect one:

| Control | Key to try after you connect |
|---|---|
| A-B loop | `Ctrl + Alt + A`, then `Ctrl + Alt + B` |
| Audio boost | `Ctrl + Alt + U` |
| Video filters | `Ctrl + Alt + F` |
| Screenshots | `Ctrl + Alt + C` |
| Timestamped notes | **Note** on the widget's Tools tab |
| Auto-skip | Switched on in Settings |

There is no sign-in inside the extension. Your account is on vcpeai.aoneahsan.com, and you press **Connect to your account** in the popup or on the Account & Sync tab in Settings to connect to it. It works on Chrome, Edge and Firefox, and [Account and sync](/docs/account-and-sync) has the steps, what connecting adds and what it records.

## A key does nothing

Letter and digit keys need `Ctrl + Alt`. Only `[`, `]`, `=`, `\`, `'`, `,`, `.` and `Esc` work alone. The full table is in [Default shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/defaults), and you can change a key in [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).
