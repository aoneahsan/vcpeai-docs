A white-label license gets you the extension and its website, running under your brand and kept up to date by us. A buyout gets you the source code.

## With a white-label license

- **The extension, under your name.** Your name, logo and colors in the popup, the widget on the page and Settings, built for Chrome, Firefox and Edge.
- **Your store listings.** We publish the extension under your own developer accounts on the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. The accounts, and any fee a store charges, are yours.
- **The companion website on your domain.** The account pages the extension connects to, deployed and hosted by us.
- **The controls Video Controls Plus has today:** speed from 0.1x to 16x, the A-B loop, audio boost to 400%, filters, screenshots, timestamped notes, rebindable keyboard shortcuts, and the extra tools on YouTube, Netflix and LinkedIn.
- **Six months of updates** from the day you buy. See [updates and renewals](./updates.md).
- **A license key and a license file** your deployment serves, so anyone can [check it](./verify.md).

A license does not include the source code, and it is not exclusive: we may license the same product to other businesses until someone buys it outright.

## With a source buyout

- **The source code of every repository that builds the extension:** the extension itself, its companion website and the backend both of them use, with the setup notes.
- **Six months of updates** from the day you buy, delivered to you, and renewals after that if you want them.
- **An end to sales.** We stop selling licenses and buyouts of Video Controls Plus. Businesses that bought a license before you keep their terms.

After a buyout you run it as you like. Hosting, store accounts and paid services you choose are your costs.

The buyout also carries a royalty: you pay us 7% of the gross revenue Video Controls Plus earns, not counting sales tax or VAT you collect. You pay it every quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as it earns revenue, and it is still owed if you sell it on. The [license terms](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms) say the same.

The full wording is in the [license terms](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms) and section 14 of the [Terms of Service](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/terms#policy-section-14).
