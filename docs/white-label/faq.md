## Do I get the source code with a license?

No. With a license we run the product under your name. The source code changes hands only with a [buyout](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).

## Which browsers does the license cover?

Chrome, Firefox and Edge. They are one platform group and are sold together.

## Is there a bundle discount?

No. Video Controls Plus is sold as one platform group, so there is nothing to bundle.

## What happens when the included updates end?

Your copy keeps running on its last version. A [renewal](./updates.md) brings updates back for 3, 6 or 12 months.

## Can another company license it too?

Yes, until someone buys it outright. After a buyout we stop selling it, and every license sold before it keeps its terms.

## Is there anything to pay after a buyout?

Yes, a royalty of 7% of the gross revenue Video Controls Plus earns, not counting sales tax or VAT you collect. You pay it every quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as it earns revenue, and it is still owed if you sell it on.

## Who pays for the store accounts and the domain?

You do. Your domain, your developer account on each store and any paid service you choose are yours.

## Can you change how it works for us?

Yes, as custom work, quoted and paid separately from the license. Tell us what you need on the [custom work page](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

## Where are the prices?

On the [pricing page](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), read live from our licensing service. Payment terms are in section 14 of the [Terms of Service](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/terms#policy-section-14).
