Every license and buyout includes six months of updates from the day you buy. When they end, your copy keeps running on its last version. Nothing switches off.

## What an update is

- New controls and fixes from Video Controls Plus, rebuilt with your name and colors.
- For a license, we publish them to your store listings on Chrome, Firefox and Edge.
- For a buyout, we deliver them to you as code.

A change made only for your copy is custom work, not an update.

## Renew

A renewal adds 3, 6 or 12 months of updates. It is counted from the later of the day you renew and your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing.

1. Open the [updates page](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) and pick a length. The price shows live.
2. Enter your license key and the email the license was bought with.
3. Press **Continue to payment**.

The key starts with `VCPEAI-WL` and is in your license email. If the page says no license matches, check both the key and the email against that email.
