A white-label license means the Video Controls Plus team runs the extension for your business under your name. Your users install your extension from the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons and Microsoft Edge Add-ons, and its companion website runs on your domain. We build it, publish it, host the website and ship the updates. You never handle the code.

There are two ways to buy, and the [white-label page](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label) compares them:

| | White-label license | Source buyout |
|---|---|---|
| Who runs it | We do, under your brand | You do |
| Source code | Stays with us | Every repository that builds the extension is handed to you |
| Can we sell it to others | Yes, until someone buys it outright | No, sales stop after a buyout |
| Updates | Six months included, then renewals | Six months included, then renewals |
| Paid after the first payment | Renewals, if you want more updates | Renewals if you want them, and a royalty of 7% of its gross revenue, paid quarterly for as long as it earns |

Video Controls Plus is sold as one platform group: the browser extension, on Chrome, Firefox and Edge together. With one group there is nothing to bundle, so no bundle discount applies.

Prices and checkout are on the [pricing page](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them live from our licensing service. The buyout has its [own page](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).
