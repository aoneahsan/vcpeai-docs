Video Controls Plus works on any site that plays a standard HTML5 video, with extra tools on YouTube, Netflix and LinkedIn. That covers Udemy, Vimeo, Twitch, Coursera, Khan Academy, Prime Video, and any other site with a standard video.

## What you get on any site

The core controls are the same everywhere: speed with `]` and `[`, the widget, and your shortcuts.

The extras made for one site are on three sites only.

| Site | Extras | Page |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | YouTube extras, each with its own switch | [YouTube](/docs/platforms/youtube) |
| Netflix | Netflix extras, off until you switch them on | [Netflix](/docs/platforms/netflix) |
| LinkedIn | LinkedIn tools that run from the menu | [LinkedIn](/docs/platforms/linkedin) |

## Embedded videos

A YouTube or Vimeo player inside an article or a course page gets the floating controls, the keyboard shortcuts and the right-click actions too, and the extension loads into an embedded frame only when the frame contains a video.

## Check a site in the popup

Open the popup on the site. It shows the site name and one of three states.

| Popup shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| Supported | The site is on the built-in list |
| Enabled | You enabled the extension on this site |
| Not enabled | The extension is not running here |

When the popup shows **Not enabled**, click **Enable on this site**. The built-in list is in Settings, on the Site Settings tab, under **Supported Sites**.

## When the controls do not appear

- The site is paused. Open the popup and click **Resume**. See [Pause per site](/docs/getting-started/pause-per-site).
- The popup shows **Not enabled**. Click **Enable on this site**.
- The page has no standard HTML5 video, so there is nothing to control.
- The widget is hidden. Press `Esc` or `Ctrl + Alt + H`.

## Related keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `]` / `[` | 0.1x faster or slower |
| `Esc` | Hide or show the widget |
| `Ctrl + Alt + H` | Hide or show the widget |

They're the same on every site, and the rest of the keys are covered in [Default shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/defaults) and in [Change a shortcut](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding).

If the controls still do not appear, go to [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
