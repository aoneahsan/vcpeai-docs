Your invitation link is on the Invitations page of your account on [vcpeai.aoneahsan.com](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com). Each new account that signs up with it and verifies its email is a confirmed invite.

## Share your link

1. Sign in on [vcpeai.aoneahsan.com](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com) with Google.
2. Open **Settings** and press **Open invitations**. On a phone, it is also in the menu as **Account / Invitations**.
3. Press **Copy link** and send it to someone who doesn't have an account yet.

## What counts

An invitation counts when the person opens your link, signs in with Google for the first time and verifies their email. The link is for new accounts only: it attaches within an hour of the account being created, and an account takes one invitation. Your own link does not count for you.

## What you earn

| For | You get today |
|---|---|
| Each confirmed invite | 10 points and an **Invitation mark** with its date |
| Every 3 confirmed invites | One month of Pro |
| A rolling year | At most 12 months of Pro from invitations |

If you already have a Pro or Team plan with no end date, a month you earn waits until that plan ends. The Invitations page lists it as **Waiting for your current plan to end**.

The page shows your confirmed count, points, marks and plan time. It never shows who signed up.

## When invitations close

We can close invitations at any time. Your earned rewards stay on the page, and new invites stop counting until invitations open again.
