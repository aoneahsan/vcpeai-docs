Floating Video Player floats the video over your other windows, and adds nothing to the page. If a floating window is the whole job, it's the lighter choice. Here picture in picture is one key and needs no account, and speed, A-B loop, filters and notes sit next to it if you want them later.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A picture-in-picture extension that floats the video over other windows while you keep browsing. <a href="https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/floating-video-player/lgjgiogmekdcfheiidohdogofdmgoaki" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Floating Video Player yourself</a>.

## One key instead of one extension

1. [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md).
2. Start a video and press `Ctrl + Alt + P`. It floats: [Watch in Picture-in-Picture](../user-guide/picture-in-picture.md).
3. On that same page there's a switch that floats the video by itself when you scroll away from it.
4. Turn Floating Video Player off on the Extensions page so one extension, not two, floats your videos. Remove it once the key does what you need.

Floating Video Player's Edge listing confirms it runs on Edge and describes the floating window. On speed, loops, filters, notes, transcripts, flashcards, history, watch later, sync, site extras, Chrome and Firefox it says nothing, so the table keeps just the one row.
