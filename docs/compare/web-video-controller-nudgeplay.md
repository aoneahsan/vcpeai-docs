NudgePlay is a small widget on the player for speed. Here the widget does a lot more, and you choose how much of it you see.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A small hover widget on the player for play, pause and speed from 0.1x to 5x, remembered per video. <a href="https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/web-video-controller-nu/dmacnkcmaabfmgjdeobmjiehgdnppipi" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Web Video Controller - NudgePlay yourself</a>.

## Move across

1. Install Video Controls Plus for your browser: [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md).
2. Pick the widget's position and size: [how the controls appear on the page](../user-guide/display-and-widget.md).
3. Change speed and go up to 16x: [Control the playback speed](../user-guide/speed-control.md). Keep a speed for each site under [Automation](../user-guide/playback-automation.md).
4. Turn Web Video Controller - NudgePlay off on your browser's Extensions page, so two extensions don't answer the same keys. Remove it once your own sites work.

Each browser keeps an extension's settings to itself, so nothing carries over. You set them once here.

## Who is better off with which

- Choose NudgePlay if you want a small on-player speed widget with per-video memory and nothing more.
- Choose Video Controls Plus if you want that widget and speed to 16x, A-B loop, filters, notes and one account across devices.
