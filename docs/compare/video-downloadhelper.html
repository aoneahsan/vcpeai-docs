Video Download Helper and Video Controls Plus

Video Download Helper saves video from many sites to your disk, on Chrome, Firefox and Edge, for one licence. Video Controls Plus doesn't download. It controls the video while you watch: speed, loops, filters, notes and watch later in one account. So there's nothing to move across. If downloading is your job, keep Video Download Helper and add us beside it.

Last checked 2026-11-07. A video downloader for Chrome, Firefox and Edge, free with a premium licence for unlimited downloads. Check Video Download Helper yourself.

Running both​

  1. Install Video Controls Plus next to it. They don't do the same thing, so there's nothing to switch off.
  2. Sometimes a download is more than you need. For a GIF or a short clip of what's on screen, Capture and export covers it.
