Super Video Speed Controller changes speed and nothing else, so there is little to move: your speed and your keys.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A speed-only extension for Chrome and Edge: presets, a custom percentage, and A, D and S keys. <a href="https://super-video-speed-controller.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Super Video Speed Controller yourself</a>.

## Move across

1. Install Video Controls Plus for your browser: [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md).
2. Press the bracket keys to change speed, or use the presets: [Control the playback speed](../user-guide/speed-control.md).
3. Their keys are A, D and S. To keep that habit, give speed up, speed down and reset those keys: [Change a keyboard shortcut](../keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding.md).
4. Turn Super Video Speed Controller off on your browser's Extensions page, so two extensions don't answer the same keys. Remove it once your own sites work.

Each browser keeps an extension's settings to itself, so nothing carries over. You set them once here.

## Who is better off with which

- Choose Super Video Speed Controller if speed is the one thing you want, with no account and nothing else on the page.
- Choose Video Controls Plus if you want speed and then a lot more: A-B loop, filters, notes, watch later and history, in one account on Chrome, Firefox and Edge.
