Pick the extension you use now. Each page has the steps to move across, or says plainly when the two do different jobs, with the prices and feature rows we read on its own pages.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** The Video Controls Plus team writes these pages, so open the sources and check us. The website's [comparison hub](https://vcpeai.aoneahsan.com/compare/extensions) has the same facts for choosing between them.

- [Video Roll](./video-roll-all-in-one-video-enhancements.md): A video toolbox extension: speed, loops, filters, picture in picture, screenshots, downloads and audio tools, free with a Pro plan.
- [Super Video Speed Controller](./super-video-speed-controller.md): A speed-only extension for Chrome and Edge: presets, a custom percentage, and A, D and S keys.
- [Video Speed Control](./video-speed-control.md): A popup speed changer for HTML5 video, 0.07x to 16x, applied with a Play button each time.
- [Floating Video Player](./floating-video-player.md): A picture-in-picture extension that floats the video over other windows while you keep browsing.
- [HTML5 Video Speed Control](./html5-video-speed-control.md): A keyboard speed controller for HTML5 video: speed up, slow down, advance and rewind.
- [Video Popout](./video-popout.md): A one-button extension that pops the playing video into a floating window with the picture-in-picture API.
- [VideoControls](./videocontrols.md): A touch-gesture player: tap to play, swipe to seek, swipe up for 2x, and auto-pause in the background.
- [Video Controls for TikTok](./video-controls-for-tiktok.md): Keys, speed from .25x to 2x, a remembered volume and pop-out windows for TikTok on the web.
- [Better YouTube Controls](./better-youtube-controls.md): A tiny YouTube-only fix: up and down always change volume, left and right always seek.
- [Web Video Controller - NudgePlay](./web-video-controller-nudgeplay.md): A small hover widget on the player for play, pause and speed from 0.1x to 5x, remembered per video.
- [Video Image Control ( new gamma )](./video-image-control-new-gamma.md): A picture-correction extension: sharpness, brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma, grayscale, sepia, rotate and flip, with speed from 0.5x to 2.5x.
- [Video Dominator](./video-dominator.md): Mouse-wheel video control: hold a key over the video to seek, change rate from 0x to 16x, set volume, and open a custom player.
- [Video Player](./video-player-extension.md): A media player extension: audio boost to 300%, speed to 8x, repeat, screenshots, local files and transcoding of formats the browser lacks.
- [Video Download Helper](./video-downloadhelper.md): A video downloader for Chrome, Firefox and Edge, free with a premium licence for unlimited downloads.
- [video downloader - CocoCut](./video-downloader-cococut.md): A downloader for Chrome and Edge that catches HLS streams and records what it can't download, free with an optional premium.
- [Video & Audio Downloader](./video-audio-downloader.md): A toolbar popup that lists the media playing in the tab for download or link copy; not for YouTube.
- [Magic Actions for YouTube](./magic-actions-for-youtube.md): A YouTube-only theatre: cinema mode with colour presets, mouse-wheel volume, auto HD, repeat, frame grabs and hidden page elements.
