With Video Speed Control you open a popup, pick a speed and press Play, every time. If you only change speed now and then, that small popup with its dark mode may be all you want. If you change it on most videos, the speed belongs on a key, and that's the whole move: two keys to learn.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A popup speed changer for HTML5 video, 0.07x to 16x, applied with a Play button each time. <a href="https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/video-speed-control/fgggaiedigajhlidjmmmengcpoipjeko" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Video Speed Control yourself</a>.

## From the popup to the bracket keys

1. [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md) in the browser you use.
2. On any video, press the bracket keys to step the speed, or type your own speed up to 16x: [Control the playback speed](../user-guide/speed-control.md).
3. The part a popup can't do: keep one speed for each site under [Automation](../user-guide/playback-automation.md), and you stop setting it at all.
4. Switch Video Speed Control off on the browser's Extensions page while you try this, then remove it once your usual sites play at the right speed.

Your popup settings stay in Video Speed Control. Browsers don't share one extension's settings with another, so you set the speeds once here.
