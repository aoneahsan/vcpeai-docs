Magic Actions works on YouTube only. Here the YouTube extras have their own switches, and the same controls work on other sites too.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A YouTube-only theatre: cinema mode with colour presets, mouse-wheel volume, auto HD, repeat, frame grabs and hidden page elements. <a href="https://www.chromeactions.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Magic Actions for YouTube yourself</a>.

## Move across

1. Install Video Controls Plus for your browser: [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md).
2. See the YouTube extras and their switches, including Auto Wide: [Video Controls Plus on YouTube](../platforms/youtube.md).
3. Loop with the [A-B loop](../user-guide/ab-loop.md) and change the picture with [video filters](../user-guide/video-filters.md); [connect your account](../account-and-sync.md) first for both.
4. Turn Magic Actions for YouTube off on your browser's Extensions page, so two extensions don't answer the same keys. Remove it once your own sites work.

Each browser keeps an extension's settings to itself, so nothing carries over. You set them once here.

## Who is better off with which

- Choose Magic Actions if YouTube is the only site you watch and you want its cinema look and 40 colour presets.
- Choose Video Controls Plus if you want YouTube extras and the same controls on Netflix, LinkedIn and any other video site, with notes and an account.
