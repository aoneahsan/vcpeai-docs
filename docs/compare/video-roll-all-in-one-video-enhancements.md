Video Roll is the nearest thing to us on this list: speed, loops and filters in a toolbox popup, free with a Pro plan. It also offers rotate, zoom, VR mode and audio tools like pitch and 3D surround in one popup, with no account. If that's what you want, and you don't need notes, stay. If you want your notes, watch later, history and settings in one account you can also open on the website, plus special features for YouTube, Netflix and LinkedIn, the move is mostly setting up the overlap again.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A video toolbox extension: speed, loops, filters, picture in picture, screenshots, downloads and audio tools, free with a Pro plan. <a href="https://videoroll.app/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Video Roll yourself</a>.

## Checking your Video Roll tools one by one

1. Open Video Roll's popup and list the tools you actually touch in a normal week.
2. [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md) and set your usual speed: [Control the playback speed](../user-guide/speed-control.md).
3. Loops and filters both need an account here, so [connect your account](../account-and-sync.md), then set up the [A-B loop](../user-guide/ab-loop.md) and [video filters](../user-guide/video-filters.md).
4. Rotate, VR mode, pitch or 3D surround on your list? Check [Visual effects and zoom](../user-guide/visual-effects.md) and [Audio tools](../user-guide/audio-tools.md) against it before you go further.
5. Turn Video Roll off on the Extensions page while you watch with ours for a few days, so the two don't both react. Remove it, and cancel Pro if you pay for it, only when nothing on your list is missing.
