Video Download Helper downloads video. We control video while you watch it, so there is no real move across: these two do different jobs.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A video downloader for Chrome, Firefox and Edge, free with a premium licence for unlimited downloads. <a href="https://downloadhelper.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Video Download Helper yourself</a>.

## Where to start

1. Install Video Controls Plus for your browser: [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md).
2. Keep it for downloads. To make a GIF or record a clip while you watch, read [Capture and export](../user-guide/capture-and-export.md).

## Who is better off with which

- Choose Video Download Helper if downloading from many sites is the job and you want it on all three browsers for one licence.
- Choose Video Controls Plus if you want to control the video while you watch it: speed, loops, filters, notes and watch later in one account.
