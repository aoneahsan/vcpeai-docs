Better YouTube Controls fixes what the arrow keys do on YouTube. Here you give the keys the jobs you want, on YouTube and elsewhere.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** A tiny YouTube-only fix: up and down always change volume, left and right always seek. <a href="https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/better-youtube-controls/bpljafjhnmghkiedehilngmbcpibahci" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Better YouTube Controls yourself</a>.

## Move across

1. Install Video Controls Plus for your browser: [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md).
2. See what runs on YouTube and turn extras on or off: [Video Controls Plus on YouTube](../platforms/youtube.md).
3. Give the arrow-key jobs to the keys you want: [Change a keyboard shortcut](../keyboard-shortcuts/rebinding.md).
4. Turn Better YouTube Controls off on your browser's Extensions page, so two extensions don't answer the same keys. Remove it once your own sites work.

Each browser keeps an extension's settings to itself, so nothing carries over. You set them once here.

## Who is better off with which

- Choose Better YouTube Controls if that one arrow-key fix on YouTube is all you want.
- Choose Video Controls Plus if you want keys you can rebind on YouTube and every other site, plus loops, notes and watch later.
