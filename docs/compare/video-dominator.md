Video Dominator runs on the mouse wheel. Our docs cover keys and the widget, not wheel actions, so this is a change of habit.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** Mouse-wheel video control: hold a key over the video to seek, change rate from 0x to 16x, set volume, and open a custom player. <a href="https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/video-dominator/hlejfomdddipabpeainiooiobjnkepoo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Video Dominator yourself</a>.

## Move across

1. Install Video Controls Plus for your browser: [Install Video Controls Plus](../getting-started/install.md).
2. Read which keys do what: [Default keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts/defaults.md).
3. Set up the widget: [how the controls appear on the page](../user-guide/display-and-widget.md).
4. If the mouse wheel is why you use it, keep Video Dominator.
5. Turn Video Dominator off on your browser's Extensions page, so two extensions don't answer the same keys. Remove it once your own sites work.

Each browser keeps an extension's settings to itself, so nothing carries over. You set them once here.

## Who is better off with which

- Choose Video Dominator if your hand stays on the mouse and you want wheel actions and a distraction-free custom player.
- Choose Video Controls Plus if you want keyboard controls, A-B loop, filters, notes, watch later and an account that keeps them.
