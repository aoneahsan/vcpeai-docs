# Who makes Video Controls Plus?

Video Controls Plus is made by Ahsan Mahmood and the Zaions team. Speed came first. It began as Udemy Video Playback Speed Controls, with a direct job: let someone set the pace of a course video. The name and reach grew, and the Chrome listing kept the same extension identity. Today the extension works on sites with standard HTML5 video and has dedicated controls for YouTube, Netflix and LinkedIn Learning.

## The idea behind the controls

Ahsan's recorded vision is one extension for the things people do around an online video player: change speed, set an A-B loop, adjust filters, keep notes and history, use flashcards and follow learning paths. The vision is broader than what every site can do today. Facebook, for example, uses the general video adapter; it does not have the same set of dedicated controls as YouTube. The extension should tell you which control works on the page in front of you, and which one needs a connected account.

Start with speed. You can change it without connecting an account. If you want the loop, notes and learning records that belong to your account, connect the extension through the website. The team keeps the difference visible in the popup and Settings, because a control that asks for an account should say so before you press it.

## How the project grew

The older Udemy name marks the first, narrower job. On 24 October 2026, version 16.3.0 reached the Chrome Web Store with the broader playback and study controls documented for that release. Firefox has its own add-on listing. Edge's 16.3.0 listing was publicly verified on 3 November 2026. Those are store milestones, not claims that every newer source change has reached every installed browser.

On 7 November 2026, Ahsan recorded the wider vision for the project: a connected place for settings, notes, learning, watch later and history, available across the browsers where people work. The website and docs give each part room for its own instructions.
