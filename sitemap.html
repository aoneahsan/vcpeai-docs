Overview

What Video Controls Plus is

Video Controls Plus is a browser extension that adds playback controls to HTML5 video on any site, with extra tools on YouTube, Netflix and LinkedIn.

AI features and what they send

Video summaries and LinkedIn writing help run on the Shared allowance, your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, or a local model. Speak runs on the Shared allowance only. Here is what each one sends.

Video Controls Plus is a browser extension for HTML5 video, built and maintained by the Video Controls Plus team.

Getting started

Install Video Controls Plus

Add Video Controls Plus from the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons or Firefox Add-ons, and speed control works as soon as it is installed.

Your first video with Video Controls Plus

Open a video, change the speed with the bracket keys, find the widget and the popup, and see which controls need a connected account.

Pause the extension on one site

Pause Video Controls Plus on a single site from the popup, for 1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day, 1 week, or until you turn it back on.

Tours on the website and the How it works page

Start a short tour of a website page from the header, jump to any step, and walk through Video Controls Plus in six steps on the How it works page.

User guide

Control the playback speed

Change video speed in 0.1x steps with the bracket keys or the widget slider up to 4x, type a custom speed up to 16x, and use 8 preset speeds.

Loop a part of a video with an A-B loop

Set point A and point B and the video replays the stretch between them, forever, once, or 1 to 20 times; the A-B loop needs an account.

Boost the audio past 100%

Raise a video's volume up to 400% and switch on normalize to even out loud and quiet parts; audio boost needs an account.

Adjust the picture with video filters

With a connected account, set brightness, contrast and saturation from 0% to 200%, use grayscale, invert or night mode, and save up to 20 presets.

Make videos easier on your eyes with Comfort

Warm the picture, make colors easier to tell apart, restyle a site's own captions and add a reading guide; Comfort needs no account and sends nothing.

Add looks and effects, and zoom the picture

With a connected account, add 20 color grades, four art looks, sharpen, vignette, film grain, a text overlay and blurred regions; Zoom & fit needs no account.

Change the balance, voice, room and loudness

With a connected account, set the left-right balance, change a voice without changing the speed, add a room, even out loudness and set a limiter; not on protected video.

Make GIFs, clips, time-lapses and contact sheets

With a connected account, make a GIF, record a clip or a time-lapse, take a screenshot burst, build a contact sheet and annotate a screenshot, all made on your device.

Let playback run on its own with Automation

Block autoplay, keep a speed per site, ramp the speed up, set reminders and pause other tabs, with no account; Up next and resume from another device need a connected account.

Set watch limits and use Focus Mode

Set a daily amount of video, limits for single sites and blocked sites, lock them with a PIN, and choose what Focus Mode hides; no account, and nothing is sent.

Study with a timer, a transcript and chapters

Run focus rounds and breaks with the Study timer, read a video's own captions as a transcript, turn them into chapters with one AI run, and practice a loop.

Save quizzes, words, diary entries and videos to your library

With a connected account, save a quiz moment, a word, a diary entry, a learning-path video or a collection item from the widget, and search everything you saved.

Share a moment and watch together

Share a link at the current time with a QR code, record a square or vertical clip, and start a watch party for up to 20 people that keeps play, pause, seeking and speed in step.

Take a screenshot of a video frame

Press Ctrl + Alt + C to save the current video frame as PNG or JPEG, download it or copy it, with an optional timestamp; it needs an account.

Take notes and add bookmarks while you watch

Save notes with the video timestamp from the widget's Tools tab, export them as Markdown, text or JSON, and add a bookmark at the current moment.

Watch in Picture-in-Picture

Press Ctrl + Alt + P to put the video in a Picture-in-Picture window, or let it enter on its own when you scroll away; it works without an account.

Skip intros, recaps and credits automatically

Auto-skip clicks the skip buttons that Netflix, Prime Video and YouTube show; it is off by default and needs an account.

Choose how the controls appear on the page

Pick the floating widget or notification only, set the widget's position, size and how it opens, and choose a theme and a language.

Keyboard shortcuts

Default keyboard shortcuts

Every default key in Video Controls Plus: letter and digit keys need Ctrl + Alt, only the bracket, punctuation and Esc keys work alone, and the optional shortcuts start with no key.

Change a keyboard shortcut

Give any shortcut another key in Settings, switch shortcuts off one by one or by section, and keep your keys across devices with a connected account.

Platforms

Video Controls Plus on YouTube

On YouTube the core controls work as on any site, and each of the YouTube extras has its own switch in Settings; most are on by default.

Video Controls Plus on Netflix

Speed and the core controls work on Netflix; the Netflix extras are off until you switch them on, and recovery keys take over in one case.

Video Controls Plus on LinkedIn

On LinkedIn the core video controls work, and a set of LinkedIn tools run from the widget or the right-click menu; switches are in Settings.

Which sites Video Controls Plus works on

Video Controls Plus works on any site that plays a standard HTML5 video, including embedded players, with extra tools on YouTube, Netflix and LinkedIn.
