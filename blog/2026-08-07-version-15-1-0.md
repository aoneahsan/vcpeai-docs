The team's other apps get a permanent home in Settings, and the promotional panel comes off the top of every Settings page, so only one banner can sit above your settings.

- **New: "More from the developer" tab.** Settings has a new tab that collects the team's other apps in one place, with a logo, a short description and a link for each. Until now the list appeared only as one step of the welcome page, so skipping setup meant losing it. The list is bundled with the extension, and nothing is fetched to show it. Opening a product is not tracked.
- **New: "More apps" in the popup.** A link at the bottom of the popup opens that tab directly.
- **Improved: a calmer Settings page.** The promotional panel that sat above every Settings page moved into the new tab, so Settings opens to your settings. A banner that does appear there stands down while the "finish your setup" reminder is showing, so two are never stacked.
- **Improved: clearer store listings.** The Chrome, Edge and Firefox listings now describe which parts of the extension link to the team's other apps, and the Chrome "contains ads" question is answered: self-promotion only, with no ad network, ad SDK or tracking involved.
