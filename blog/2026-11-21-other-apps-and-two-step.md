Your account on the website has two new pages: **Other apps**, for tools you connect to your own library, and **Security**, for a second step after Google.

- **New: access keys.** Make a key on **Other apps** for a script or a spreadsheet sync, tick what it reaches, and use it with the API. [Access keys](/docs/developers/access-keys).
- **New: assistants.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code or Cursor and ask about your notes, bookmarks and flashcards. Anything an assistant asks to delete waits on the website for your yes. [Assistants](/docs/developers/assistants).
- **New: webhooks.** Your server gets a short signed message the moment you save a note or a bookmark. What you wrote stays out of it unless you tick **Include what was written**. [Webhooks](/docs/developers/webhooks).
- **New: two-step sign-in.** A six-digit code from your phone after Google, ten recovery codes, and **Remember this browser for 7 days** if you tick it. With it on, the extension stays connected for 7 days at a time, then asks you to connect again. [Two-step sign-in](/docs/developers/two-step-sign-in).

Keys, assistants and webhooks are on every plan, Free included. Everything for developers is in [Video Controls Plus for developers](/docs/developers).
